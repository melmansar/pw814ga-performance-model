This model will predict the stagnation properties at every station of the P&WC PW814GA engine as well as its
static properties at the nozzle exits at design and off design conditions. It will also predict engine
thrust, fuel flow, and specific fuel consumption.

It will use PW814GA data from ICAO databank issue 32B UID 01P22PW141 and EASA TCDS IM.E.096 Issue 07 in order
to tune turbine inlet temperature and inlet air mass flow to match takeoff thrust and fuel flow, and then
validate the model against the climbout (85% thrust), approach (30% thrust), and idle (7% thrust) fuel flow values.
All other component values will be fixed at typical textbook values with stated ranges.

For validation, a tolerance of 5%, 10%, and 20% will be used respectively for the 85%, 30%, and 7% thrust levels.
The generic maps the model will use are less accurate further from the design point which is 100% thrust for
takeoff, so tolerances get looser as we move further from it.

This model will use sea level data points to calibrate and validate. While it may be used to predict cruise
conditions, the validity of these results will not be verified. Additionally, the model will only predict
the operation of an uninstalled engine, as this was the condition used to obtain the calibration and validation
data, and thus it will not consider things like bleed air for the cabin and power taken off for generators.
