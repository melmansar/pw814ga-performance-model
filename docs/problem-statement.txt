This model will predict the stagnation properties of the P&WC PW814GA engine as well as its static properties at the nozzle exits. It will also predict engine thrust, fuel flow, and specific fuel consumption.
It will use PW814GA data from ICAO databank issue 32B UID 01P22PW141 and EASA TCDS IM.E.096 Issue 07 in order to calibrate turbine inlet temperature and another unknown around takeoff values for fuel flow and thrust and then validate the model against the climbout (85% thrust), approach (30% thrust), and idle (7% thrust) fuel flow values.
All other component values will be fixed at typical textbook values with stated ranges.
For validation, a tolerance of 5%, 10%, and 20% will be used respectively for the 85%, 30%, and 7% thrust levels. The generic maps the model will use are less accurate further from the design point which is 100% thrust for takeoff, so tolerances get looser as we further from it.
This model will use sea level data points to calibrate and validate. While it may be used to predict cruise conditions, the validity of these results will not be verified. Additionally, the model will only predict the operation of an uninstalled engine, and thus will not consider things like bleed air for the cabin, power taken off for generators. Calibration and validation data was taken from uninstalled engine tests, thus it will be modeled that way too.
