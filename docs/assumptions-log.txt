3. MODELING ASSUMPTIONS
-----------------------

(To be filled in as the model is built.)

* ITT is not turbine inlet temperature. TIT is higher than ITT because
  the turbine extracts work and cooling air mixes in. The 965 C limit
  is used as a check: the calibrated model's temperature at the ITT
  station at takeoff must be at or below 965 C.
* The databank point is at standard day sea level (15 C), below the
  33 C ambient limit, so the engine runs with temperature margin there.
  ITT is not set equal to 965 C at the design point.
*Fan pressure ratio is fixed at a textbook value with a range, source to be added. 
 The overall pressure ratio is 33.56 from the databank and can be used to compute
 the compressor pressure ratio.
